FAQ

Will this fit my Kubota tractor?

It is made for the mid PTO (power take-off) on the Kubota B2150, B1550 and B1750. It was test-fitted on a B2150, and the B1550 and B1750 use the same mid PTO case. The B2150 stub measured about 1 in across with about 1.75 in sticking out, and the cover has a 1.5 in bore and 2.5 in of inside depth, so there is plenty of clearance. Please verify fit on your own tractor before use.

How do I install it? Do I need to drill anything?

No drilling. It uses the factory bottom bolt hole. With the engine off and the key out, remove the bottom bolt, set the cover over the stub, then thread in whichever included bolt fits by hand, with the washer, and snug it.

Why does the kit come with two different bolts?

So it fits either thread. You get one 5/16-18 and one M8 x 1.25 stainless steel bolt. Use whichever one threads in by hand. Never force a bolt or re-tap the hole.

What are the magnets for?

The 5 built-in magnets on the mounting face add holding force. The bolt plus the magnets keep the cover from moving or shifting.

What is it made of, and will it hold up outdoors?

The cover is 3D printed in ASA, a UV-resistant engineering plastic chosen for outdoor use. A specific temperature rating is not published yet. If you have a heat question for your setup, message us on eBay before you buy.

What's included in the kit?

One dust cover with 5 built-in magnets, two stainless steel bolts (5/16-18 and M8 x 1.25) and one stainless steel washer that spreads the load. The photos show the single cover from two angles.

Can I leave it on when I use a mid-mount mower or other mid PTO implement?

No. Remove the cover before using a mid PTO implement. It is a dust cover only, not a safety shield.
